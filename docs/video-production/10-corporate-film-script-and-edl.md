## VOICEOVER RECORDING GUIDE

**Option A — Professional VO artist (recommended)**
- Use Voices.com, Voice123, or Fiverr (search "Nigerian English corporate VO" or "West African English corporate")
- Brief: slow, authoritative, documentary tone. NOT sales-y. Think BBC documentary narrator.
- Send them the script above exactly as written.
- Request: MP3 + WAV, 44.1kHz, no background music, no reverb.

**Option B — AI voiceover**
- ElevenLabs.io → choose a deep male voice (e.g. "Daniel" or "Liam")
- Paste the script, set stability ~70, similarity ~60, style ~20
- Download as MP3

**Option C — Record yourself**
- Quiet room, phone or USB mic
- Read slowly — slower than feels natural
- Record 3 takes, use the most confident one
