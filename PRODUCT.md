# PRODUCT.md — Enikkom Construction Limited corporate site

register: brand

## What this is

The public corporate site of Enikkom Construction Limited (ECL) and its trenchless
delivery arm HDDTEC — an indigenous Nigerian EPC contractor founded 1995,
specializing in HDD trenchless crossings, pipeline construction, dredging, piling
and marine civil works for oil & gas operators.

## Users

- **Procurement and tender teams** at operators (SPDC, NNPC, Dangote, Saipem…)
  prequalifying contractors: they need capability proof, project records,
  certifications, equipment schedules, and a credible contact route.
- **EPC partners** evaluating JV/subcontract capability.
- **Engineers/candidates** checking careers and company standing.

They are skeptical, time-poor professionals. The site's job is to read like an
audited record, not a marketing funnel.

## Brand personality

Corporate, industrial, audit-ready, investor-facing. Shell/Saipem/Bechtel-class
discipline. Quiet confidence carried by verifiable facts (16" × 3.1 km,
named clients, ISO certs), never adjectives.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (gradient heroes, floating badges, icon grids).
- AI-generated look: neon glows, glassmorphism, blobs, "Learn more" CTAs,
  robotic superlative copy.
- Anything playful, startup-flavored, or visually loud.

## Strategic principles

1. **Nothing invented.** Every fact traces to the client's own documents
   (`resources/eclweb/`). Unverifiable claims get removed, not polished.
2. **Proof over promise.** Named projects with dimensions beat capability prose.
3. **Shell-style restraint.** Light surfaces, one brand-blue accent (#1050A0),
   modest type, whitespace over boxes.
4. **Procurement-grade UX.** Fast paths to the project register, capability
   statement PDF, and one honest contact route.
